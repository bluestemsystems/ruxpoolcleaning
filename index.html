Questions

What areas do you serve?

New Braunfels and nearby parts of Comal County. If you are just outside town, ask anyway and we will let you know.

Are chemicals included?

No. Rux does not supply chemicals. We test your water every visit. If you keep chemicals on hand, our Chemical Test and Rebalance add-on ($35) uses them to balance your water. Green pool recovery already includes adding your chemicals.

What if my green pool takes more than two visits?

Most green pools clear in two visits. If yours needs one more, it is $45. If it needs more than that, we will quote the price with you before doing the work.

Do you do repairs?

No. We strictly clean, so no equipment repairs, filling pools or electrical work. If we spot an equipment problem during a visit, we will let you know so you can call a repair company.

What types of filters do you service?

We service pools with cartridge, DE and sand filters. Cartridge filter cleaning is included with weekly service, and for one-time and green pool jobs it starts at $75, based on how many cartridges your filter holds. Sand filters are cleaned at no charge, but we do not replace sand. We do not service salt cells.

Do I need to be home?

No. We just need access to the pool and equipment. Please keep the gate unlocked or share the code, and keep pets inside on your service day. If we cannot get in, a return trip is $45.

What happens with bad weather or holidays?

Your visit may shift by a day. We will text you if it does.

Is there a contract?

Weekly service runs month to month with a simple service agreement. Either of us can cancel with 30 days' notice, and a text or email is fine.

How do I pay?

Weekly service is billed monthly in advance. The first month is due at signup, then it drafts automatically from your card or bank account on the 1st. One-time cleans and add-ons are due when the work is done. Green pool recovery is paid in full before the first visit. Payments more than 10 days late have a $15 late fee.
