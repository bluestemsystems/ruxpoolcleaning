Questions

What areas do you serve?

New Braunfels and nearby parts of Comal County. If you are just outside town, ask anyway and we will let you know.

Do I need to be home?

No. We just need access to the pool and equipment. Please keep gates unlocked and pets inside on your service day. If we cannot get in, a return trip is $45.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans run month to month with a simple service agreement. You can cancel with written notice.

What chemicals are included in full service?

Standard sanitizer and balancing chemicals for routine weekly care. Extra treatments for algae, storms or heavy use are billed at cost plus 20% and listed on your visit report.

How do I pay?

Monthly plans are billed on autopay. One-time services are invoiced after the visit. Payments more than 10 days late have a $15 late fee.
